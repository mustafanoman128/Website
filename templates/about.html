About me

I am a data analyst and AI engineer who turns complex, messy datasets into decisions that actually move the needle. My work sits at the intersection of business intelligence, machine learning, and applied AI, and I am genuinely obsessed with what becomes possible when you point the right tools at the right problem.

My career has taken me through serious data environments across the US. At Pregis, I built executive Tableau dashboards tracking 75+ KPIs and drove a 17% lift in reporting engagement. At Stericycle, I engineered outbound campaign workflows that contributed $2.3M in revenue. At The Henry Ford, I designed a fuzzy matching algorithm that improved data accuracy by 40% across 500,000 records and saved roughly $10,000 per month in geocoding costs. The stakes were real, and the data was never clean.

Today I work independently, building end-to-end AI and data products. That includes an ML-powered property pricing model with an R² of 0.85, a full-stack personal finance app with 11 production-ready features, and AI-assisted workflows that compress weeks of analysis into hours. Large language models in particular fascinate me, and I spend a significant amount of my own time exploring what they can do when applied to real business problems.

My academic foundation comes from LUMS, where I studied Economics and learned to care about why numbers move, not just what they say. A Master's in Business Analytics from the University of Illinois at Chicago built out the technical side. The combination puts me in a useful place: comfortable with the math, fluent in the business context, and always pushing toward what AI makes possible next.
