Pakistan Property
Price Estimator

An AI system that tells you what a property is worth. Not a single number, but a calibrated range: a low estimate, a likely price, and a ceiling. Built for developers, agents, and investors across five Pakistani cities who need to stop pricing on instinct. Trained on 120,000+ Zameen.com listings, it returns a valuation in seconds — with inflation adjustment, rental yield analysis, EMI calculations, and multi-property comparison built in.

120K+ Training Listings
5 Cities Covered
P10–P90 Confidence Range
How It Works
  • Three LightGBM models (P10, P50, P90) deliver a price range, not a false point estimate
  • Neighbourhood-level encoding across 10,000+ Pakistani locations with fuzzy autocomplete
  • KMeans geo-clustering and distance-to-city-center as spatial pricing features
  • Time-based train/test split to prevent data leakage across listing dates
  • FastAPI backend with CPI-adjusted 2026 pricing and live rental yield output
