Pakistan Property
Price Estimator

AI-powered property valuation for Pakistan's residential market. Three LightGBM quantile regression models trained on 120,000+ Zameen.com listings deliver P10/P50/P90 confidence intervals — so you see a price range, not a false point estimate. Features 10,000+ neighborhood autocomplete with fuzzy matching, inflation adjustment (2019 → 2026 CPI), an EMI calculator, rental yield analysis, and multi-property comparison across Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, and Faisalabad.

120K+ Training Listings
5 Cities Covered
P10–P90 Confidence Range
Key Techniques
  • LightGBM quantile regression (3 models: P10, P50, P90) for calibrated price ranges
  • Neighbourhood-level target encoding across 10,000+ Pakistani locations
  • KMeans geo-clustering + distance-to-city-center as spatial features
  • Fuzzy location matching via rapidfuzz for typo-tolerant autocomplete
  • Time-based train/test split to prevent data leakage
  • FastAPI backend with inflation-adjusted 2026 pricing output
